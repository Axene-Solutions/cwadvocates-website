Privacy Notice

C&W Advocates, in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya. Last updated 30 October 2026.

What we collect

When you use our contact form we collect your name, email address, telephone number (optional), the practice area you select, and the message you write. If you contact us on WhatsApp, we see your number and the messages you send.

Why we use it

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry, to assess whether we can act for you, and to arrange a consultation. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form.

Please do not send sensitive details

The form is for an initial enquiry. Please do not include confidential documents, financial account details or sensitive personal information. We will tell you how to share those safely once we have spoken.

Who handles it

Form submissions pass through Formspree, our form service provider, and reach us by email. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our domain is served through Cloudflare. These providers process data on our behalf. We do not sell your personal data.

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It is designed to measure traffic without using cookies or tracking individuals across sites.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to deal with your request and meet our professional and legal obligations. If we do not act for you, we will delete the details when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, ask us to delete it, object to our use of it, or withdraw your consent at any time. To do this, email info@cwadvocates.co.ke.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

This notice is a working draft. Please have the partners review it before relying on it.
