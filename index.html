About C&W Advocates

C&W Advocates is a Nairobi-based law firm built around direct partner involvement in every matter we take on. We believe clients are best served by lawyers who are genuinely accessible: who return calls, explain the reasoning behind their advice, and treat each file as more than a number.

Our approach

We start by listening. Before recommending a strategy, we make sure we understand what the client is actually trying to achieve, not just the immediate legal question but the practical outcome that matters to them. We then set out the realistic options, the likely costs and timelines, and a recommended path, so clients can make informed decisions at every stage.

Our values

  • IntegrityHonest advice, even when it isn't what a client hopes to hear.
  • DiligenceCareful preparation on every file, regardless of size.
  • AccessibilityClients can reach the lawyer handling their matter, not just a call centre.
  • ValueTransparent fees agreed upfront, with no surprises.
